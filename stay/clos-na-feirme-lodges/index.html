Once part of the grand Ashford Estate (3km), Clos Na Feirme Holiday Village is a development of 2, 3 and 4 bedroom holiday lodges just 1.5Km from the village of Clonbur Co. Galway. On extensive grounds, the houses are located at the foot of Mount Gable, overlooking Lough Corrib and Lough Mask. Each house has been carefully designed using interior designers and colour analysts, all have large halls and balcony areas and large open living spaces. The houses are fully fitted to a high specification to make the most of the interior spaces as well as the natural beauty of the surrounding location. Most are centred around a stone courtyard with stunning views over Lough Corrib. The development is designed around a tree lined avenue and landscaped ground and a stonework water feature. On the doorstep of Cong and Ashford Castle, and set on the beautiful shores of Lough Corrib, staying here brings you to the heart of Connemara’s Gaeltacht region. Clos Na Feirme Holiday Village lies only 1.5km from the village of Clonbur, where you will find many typical Irish Pubs, Restaurants and Shops. The village lies close to the town of Cong and the Mayo border. You are close to Knock Airport, Sligo and Galway City. Activities you can enjoy in the area include archery, horse riding, football, trout angling is very popular in the area due to its location between Lough Corrib and Lough Mask. There are several looped and linear walks around Clonbur and Clos na Feirme, with the nearest one being one of the most scenic. Suiloid Ben Sleibhe (Mount Gable Walk) is an 8km walk along the old road linking Clonbur to the village of Corr na Mona. With stunning views over Lough Corrib, and featuring historical points of interest along the route, it is the ideal starting point for your Connemara holiday. The property is just minutes away from the village of Cong, where the world famous Ashford Castle is located. On the doorstep here activities include taking a Corrib Cruise, or a pony and trap tour of The Quiet Man movie sites. A short 40 minute drive brings to the bustling town of Westport, or the more quiet sedate Kylemore Abbey, home to the Benedictine Nuns and an oasis of tranquillity.
